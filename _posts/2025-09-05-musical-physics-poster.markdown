# Musical Physics: Lessons from building music apps in Unity

We built a music app for iOS in the Unity game engine.
An instrument, midi device and fun toy.

Why Unity?
- Graphics good
- Physics simulation - bouncy things
- Targeting spatial platforms

## The Upsides

Super easy spatial mixing

Sample playback with high numbers of voices

Very fast interaction development 


## The Downsides

Performance optimised to visuals not music

Easy to degrade audio quality - check those sample rates

Not easy to run as a plugin
